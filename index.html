Design Process

A practical workflow that keeps the user, product goals and visual consistency connected from the first idea to the final interface.

01

Understand

Define the problem, user needs and business context before moving into interface decisions.

02

Explore

Research references, map opportunities and generate several possible directions.

03

Structure

Create flows, wireframes and information architecture to make the experience easy to follow.

04

Design

Build high-fidelity screens, components and interaction patterns with visual consistency.

05

Validate

Prototype, test and refine based on clarity, accessibility and usability feedback.

06

Launch & iterate

Support handoff, launch and future improvements with a maintainable design system.
